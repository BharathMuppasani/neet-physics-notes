Fluids syllabus limit

Fluids is examined in schedule order "up to 9.6.5 Capillarity, determination of surface tension by capillary rise method". That is the whole chapter: pressure → Bernoulli → viscosity → surface tension → capillarity.

What are Fluids I and Fluids II?

Both are the same chapter (Mechanical Properties of Fluids), split in two because it is long. The split follows your module tests:

  • Fluids I covers pressure, Pascal, buoyancy, continuity, Bernoulli and Torricelli. That is Test-05.
  • Fluids II covers viscosity, Stokes' law, surface tension, bubbles and capillarity. That is Test-06.
